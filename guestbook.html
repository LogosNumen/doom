signatures · unattended · collected by hand

There is no server behind this site. Nothing here can take a message and hold it — it is flat files on a static host, which is why the station has no contact form and no address on the footer.

So the book takes the long way round. Signing opens a pre-filled message you send yourself — it lands in the site's issue tracker, which is the only inbox this thing has. Nothing then copies it out by hand: the station picks the tracker up on its own and writes the entry into the book. Give it a minute and refresh.
