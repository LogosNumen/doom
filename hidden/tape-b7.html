600 – leaderand then the machine kept turning, because nobody was in the room to stop it

This is the reel I go back to. Not for the four seconds — for the two hundred counter units either side of them, which are nothing, and which somebody sat through.

The nine groups at 402 are the whole message the station has. Not a message it received: a message it sends, every night, and has sent since before the log starts. Nine groups is not much. You can hold nine groups in your head if you are careful.

Take the offset off. Do not add it. Everybody adds it.
