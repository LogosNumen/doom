So: nine groups, two digits each that mean anything, one number taken off them, and it spells the least mysterious thing it could possibly have spelled.

I did think about making it a better word. Something with more in it. I sat with a list for an evening and everything I liked was a word about radios, and the station is not about radios, and the two people who have got this far did not do it for a word about radios.

It says what it says because that is the whole content of the transmission and always has been. A machine nobody is at, saying still here, on a frequency nobody is on, every night, correctly, for years.

── four racks, three of them off

── one duty log that stops on a Tuesday
── no procedure for shutting any of it down

That is the end of the station. There is nothing further up the hill.

There is one room left, and it is not part of the transmission. Somebody has to sit out a forty-minute dwell with nothing on the channel, and they did it by reading. The shelf in the hut is empty — you bring your own.
