behind the door nobody logged

There are four racks in the hut. Three of them are switched off at the wall and have been since before I had keys.

The fourth is not connected to anything. Both ends. I have traced the loom twice, once with a meter and once with my hands in the dark because the light in there is on the same circuit as the three that are off.

It hums.

Fifty cycles, and the harmonics you would expect, and one component at about eleven hertz that is not a harmonic of anything and that I have decided, in writing, on this page, not to identify.

── it is not the mains. the mains are off.
── it is not the mast. I have stood under the mast.
── it is louder in february
── it is the same note as the carrier
── it has never once stopped

You can have it. It is only arithmetic — two oscillators and a filter, same as everything else on this site. But it is the right arithmetic, and if you put it on quietly and leave it on, after about twenty minutes you will start doing what I do, which is checking whether it is still there.

Since you are down here: the thing people write in about is the numbers, and the thing they get stuck on is that they add the offset instead of taking it off. The register is R3. It is in the manual, under Registers, where a register would be.
