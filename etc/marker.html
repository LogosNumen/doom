A buzzer, once a second, on an empty channel. It is the least interesting sound the station makes and it is the one I would miss.

A marker exists so that anyone sweeping the band knows the frequency is held. It is not a message and it is not addressed to anybody. It says occupied, over and over, to a band nobody is sweeping.

ＷＨＡＴ　ＩＴ　ＩＳ
── one pulse a second, ±40 ms, never drifting further
── 830 Hz, square, no modulation on it
── fourteen counter units of it on the reel, running normally
── it stops for the groups, and starts again after
── it has never once been acknowledged

The last item is the only one I check. A marker is a question in the sense that a doorbell is a question, and the answer to a doorbell is somebody coming to the door. Nobody has ever come to this door. I have the log and the reel and six years of my own handwriting saying so.

It goes on anyway. It went on before the log starts and it will go on after whoever is reading this has closed the tab, and the fact that neither of us is doing anything about it is the whole arrangement.
