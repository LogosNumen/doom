ＮＯＴ　ＩＮ　ＴＨＩＳ　ＣＯＰＹ

The manual runs continuously from page one to the end. There is no gap in the numbering, so this was not torn out of my copy — it was never in any copy.

I have reconstructed what the steps would have to be, from the other procedures, which all assume this one exists and refer to it. Work down them. Each one is a thing you would have to be able to do.

  1. There is no controlling authority named anywhere in the building. The letterhead on the manual is a printer's specimen — the same three words in four different weights.
  2. The log has a column for the first transmission of a watch and none for the last. Six years of it and I only noticed writing this.
  3. Already done, and not by me. There has been no modulation on this carrier in the whole time I have been coming up here. You cannot stop a thing that is not happening.
  4. R0 is sealed at 1 and the manual notes, in the same flat voice it uses for everything, that it has never been 0. The meter is not connected. There is nothing to confirm it at.
  5. Three racks are already off at the wall. The fourth is not connected at either end and hums regardless, so isolating the supply would isolate it from nothing.
  6. The chain was cut long before I had keys — you can tell from the rust on the cut. I have never brought a replacement. I have thought about it every single time and never once put one in the car.
  7. To whom.
  8. —

That is as far as it goes.

Seven things that cannot be done and an eighth that is not written down, and the eighth is the one that would actually stop it.

I do not think the procedure was lost. I think whoever assembled the manual got to this page, understood what it would have to say, and put the binder down.

The lamp is still doing forty a minute. It is supposed to be thirty. Nobody is coming to fix that either, and I have stopped describing it as a fault.
