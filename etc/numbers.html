ＴＲＡＮＳＭＩＳＳＩＯＮ　ＦＯＲＭＡＴ
── preamble ....... 12 s tone
── call ........... three times
── count .......... group count, twice
── message ........ 9 groups of 5
── repeat ......... 2 passes, identical
── close .......... "out", then carrier

ＧＲＯＵＰ　ＳＴＲＵＣＴＵＲＥ　　2 – 1 – 2
── the first two digits carry the payload
── the middle digit is padding
── the last two digits are padding

payload runs 01–26 after correction.
the correction is a fixed offset held in a register.
it is not printed here. it is printed where offsets
are printed, in the manual.

idle
