one bit · local only

This page can show you what the camera on this machine sees, rendered at one bit per pixel through an ordered dither, which is roughly what you would look like if you were being transmitted by something built in 1971.

It does not ask for the camera until you press the button. Nothing is uploaded, recorded, or sent anywhere. There is no server behind this page — the frames go from the camera into a canvas and are thrown away sixty times a second. Close the tab and it is gone. You can check: the whole of it is in the source of this file.

The browser will only allow a camera over https or on localhost. Opened from a disk, this page will refuse, and that refusal is the browser being sensible.
