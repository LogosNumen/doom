Every site had one of these. Mine came with the template and I never took it off, and at some point I noticed it had never once moved.

I checked the obvious things. It is not pointed at a script. There is no script for it to be pointed at. It is an image tag with nothing behind it, drawing a number that was baked in when the page was made, and it has been reporting that number to everybody who has ever loaded it.

Which makes it the most accurate instrument in the building. The meter on the front panel is not connected either, and that one at least has the decency to twitch.

The second number is the honest one, and it only exists on your machine. Nobody else can see it, I cannot see it, and nothing sends it anywhere. It is a counter for an audience of one, which is the correct size for an audience here.
